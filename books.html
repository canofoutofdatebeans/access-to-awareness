The Books

Words to sit with, slowly.

Two books, written the way I teach — with clarity, and without a single wasted word.

The Elegance of Simplicity

A Wisdom Teacher's Epic Journey to Consciousness

Two women meet across a lifetime — one still seeking, one who has arrived. Through their story, the book moves gently through human nature, the ego, and the transcendence that waits beyond it. It is not a manual. It is a journey you take alongside them.

Available as a paperback, an e-book, and an audiobook — and in French as L'Élégance de la Simplicité.

Read on Amazon Édition française →
Awakening All Sleepwalkers

An Essential Guide to Expand Awareness

A book for those who suspect they have been asleep, and are ready to wake. It meets, with honesty, our quiet resistance to consciousness — and offers a clear path toward a new culture for humankind. Not enlightenment. Just waking up.

Available as a hardcopy, on Kindle, and as a downloadable PDF — with free worksheets to walk beside it.
