The Work

Four ways to begin.

Every path leads to the same place — the power of your own awareness. Where you enter depends only on you.

01

The Master Course

Ten two-hour sessions, one to one, on Zoom — every week or two, and recorded for you to keep. Together we trace, session by session, the ego you have been constructing since the age of two: the beliefs, the borrowed judgments, the survival mechanisms that quietly run a life. I teach the way the old masters did — by question, not by answer — so that what you find, you find for yourself. This is the heart of the work, and my main offering.

One to one10 × 2 hoursOn ZoomRecorded

02

Mentoring

One-hour sessions, one to one, taken singly or in packages, weekly or every other week. This is for students who want to go further — to meet their patterns in real time, in relationship, in work, in leadership, in the turning-points of a life. Most begin after the Master Course. The depth of your commitment shapes the depth of your transformation.

03

Awakening All Sleepwalkers — the free course

A live course, given freely, opened afresh every four months. It is the clearest doorway into awareness I know, and the place most students begin. If you suspect you have been asleep and are ready to look — this is your first step. Come as you are.
