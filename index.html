An invitation

I will not hand you the truth, and I will not try to fix you. What I do promise is this: clarity, an understanding of your own mechanisms, the power of real choice, and the remembering of who you actually are.

I hold up a mirror, with love and a good deal of humour, until you can no longer look away. What follows is the slow disentanglement of the ego, and the return of a life that is Soul-expressed. This is not a walk in the park. It is extraordinary.

You cannot escape the mirror. I say this with love.

About Sophie

French by birth, formed in the universities of France, undone and remade by an awakening at the age of twelve. I have led seminars for tens of thousands of people across more than twenty countries, and then walked away from all of it.

Today I live simply in Menorca, close to the sea and the silence, and I teach the only thing I know to be true.
