The Live Call

Every Wednesday, we practise together.

One hour, live and free, to practise awareness — the ultimate power. No cost, and no requirement. Only your presence.

What it is

A weekly hour of awareness.

In these times of upheaval and uncertainty, I feel called to offer my work freely. So each week we gather, live, for a single hour — and we practise.

We take up a question, we look at it honestly, together, and we leave a little more awake than we arrived. Come as you are. Come every week, or come just once. There is nothing to prepare, and nothing to prove.

This is the simplest way to sit in the room with me.
