An invitation

I work with students who are quietly certain there is something more than the routine we call life — and are hungry enough to look for it.

I promise you nothing, and I fix no one. I hold up a mirror — with love, and a good deal of humour — until you can no longer look away. What follows is the slow disentanglement of the ego, and the return of a life that is Soul-expressed.

This is not a walk in the park. It is extraordinary.

You cannot escape the mirror. I say this with love.

About Sophie

French by birth, formed in the universities of England and France, undone and remade by a spiritual awakening at the age of twelve. Over the years I have sat with more than eighty thousand people across the world.

Today I live simply in Menorca — close to the sea, and to the silence — and I teach the only thing I know to be true.
